How to Play

  1. Choose 2–6 teams and set an assignment checkpoint. A team earns a turn whenever its students complete that checkpoint.
  2. Click the team on the scoreboard, then shuffle. Turn on Play in team order to select the next team automatically from top to bottom. With this option off, click a team before each shuffle. On 1–4, click that many squares. A 5 lets you shield one of your squares, with no new claims. Shields stay until opponents spend claims to remove them. Teams can collect multiple shields, even on the same square; each shield absorbs one claim before that square can be stolen. A 6 is a bust: that turn ends with no claims.
  3. Each claimed square must share an edge with the active team's territory. You may claim an empty square or steal a neighboring team's square. Diagonals do not count.
  4. A team's final square can never be stolen. Every team can keep playing.
  5. Use the optional timer or end the game whenever you like. The team with the most squares wins.
